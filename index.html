FAQ

Do I need to buy new software?

Usually not. I start with what you already have and only recommend new tools when they’re clearly worth it.

We’re a small organization. Is this worth it?

Small teams often benefit most, because every hour saved on manual work goes back into your mission or your customers.

Is my data safe?

I follow security best practices, work inside your existing systems wherever possible, and will sign a confidentiality agreement.

How much does it cost?

It depends on scope. Most clients start with a fixed-price assessment, so you know exactly what you’re paying before committing to anything larger.
